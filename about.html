About SANSKRITI NX

Preserving Heritage, Crafting Beauty

Our Story

SANSKRITI NX was born from a deep passion for preserving and promoting the rich heritage of Indian handicrafts. Based in Raipur, the cultural heart of Chhattisgarh, we have dedicated ourselves to showcasing the finest examples of traditional metalwork and tribal artistry.

Our journey began with a simple yet powerful vision: to bridge the gap between traditional artisans and modern homes, ensuring that the age-old techniques and cultural stories embodied in each piece continue to inspire and beautify living spaces across the world.

Every item in our collection is handpicked and crafted by master artisans who have inherited their skills through generations. From the intricate details of brass Ganesha idols to the rustic charm of Dhokra tribal sculptures, each piece carries the soul of its creator and the spirit of India's artistic traditions.

Our Mission

To celebrate and preserve India's rich handicraft heritage by connecting skilled artisans with art lovers worldwide, ensuring that traditional craftsmanship continues to thrive in the modern world while bringing authentic beauty and cultural depth into every home.
